# Tamil meme template references

These links were collected as *search starting points* for ideas. **No image from these sites is
bundled in the app.** The 20 bundled templates (`assets/memes/tm001.png` ... `tm020.png`) are original
illustrations produced by `tool/generate_meme_templates.py`, drawn in the mood of each idea. Their
actor / source fields describe the comedy style or scene that inspired the caption idea; they do not mean
the picture shows that person.

## Using real film stills

Film stills and screenshots of actors are copyrighted, and a person's image may also carry publicity
rights. Before shipping any, get written permission or a licence. Keep unverified images in the
git-ignored `reference_images/` folder and do not list them in `pubspec.yaml`.
